About me

1. Raspberry Pi’s and Arduino#

I love working with tiny computers like a small RaPi Zero, and playing around with it, to get the most of it’s functionality! Especially with Arduino’s I have played around a lot, doing fun little projects in my free-time.

I’d be happy to be taught more about them, through a apprenticeship (Lehre) and develop my skills even further.

2. Photography and Filmmaking#

I love photography so much, that I even created a own subpage just for this! I would say, photography is more of a hobby, but at the same time I am quite knowledgeable.

If I had to choose a favorite subcategory, I would say Portrait Photography, because this is where I can really tweak to get the most out of a persons visuals. Another topic that really fascinated me since a long time is Space Photography’ and Experimental Photography. So for those who have a deeper understanding with this, it becomes quite obvious that I love to play with light.

With Filmmaking, I have not made a lot of personal experience yet, beside helping out a friend of mine, that studies Filmmaking near Brussels. I do have a deep understanding, when it comes to editing a film. Especially shortfilms, with their short story they tell, which typically points to a much larger topic, I’ve had a lot of fun already.

3. Graphics Design#

Another key aspect of my skills, is graphics design, which I had as a subject in my school for several years. Especially the thought about how a company presents itself through their flyers, products and their Product Design is really appealing to me and I would love to help out there!

So far I have had my schools projects, in which I even ended up taking the oral exam as part of my Matura. Another key part, is when I took part in a workshop, to create a calendar for students all over Austria.

([Link to the Calendar][https://cms.amazone.or.at/media/pages/projekte/rebell-innen-kalender/2ca0306e32-1755522213/kalender2025_26_finalweb.pdf]).

4. Mechatronics#

This goes hand in hand, with my Raspberry Pi and Arduino projects, and roots back to my early youth, in which I took part in the “Robotik”-course of our school. Within this course, we’ve built our own robots out of Lego (from the EV3 series) and our teacher guided us programming it ourselves. In the end of another 3 years of this course, I had a small robot, that could perfectly follow a line, on a white canvas, in any environment, which wasn’t even completely helpless, if the line ended entirely. It even had obstacle avoidance!!

Concluding Mechatronics resembles another of my key interests, in which I would love to advance and learn more! Even nowadays I love taking broken computers apart and repurposing those parts, to further serve a useful job, making our lives easier or simply more comfortable.

5. Philosophy#

Ever since the first time we had it in school, I loved it. It even went as far as taking part in a project (Philosophie Olympiade) within my school, where I got the opportunity to express my thoughts about certain philosophical projects.

I really enjoy talking about it, with people who are interested as well. So if you want to chat, just ask me!
